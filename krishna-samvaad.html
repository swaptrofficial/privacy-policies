Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Krishna Samvaad. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect information when you use our application and related services.

Krishna Samvaad is an AI-powered reflection and wellness application inspired by philosophical teachings from the Bhagavad Gita.

2. Information We Collect

Krishna Samvaad does not require account creation or user login.

We do not permanently collect, store, or maintain personal chat history on our servers.

When you submit a query within the application, the text you enter is temporarily processed through our backend infrastructure and shared with trusted AI service providers solely for the purpose of generating a response.

Conversation history and saved reflections are stored locally on your device for your personal use and are not synced to our servers.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Personal identity information
  • Device contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Precise device location
  • Payment information
  • User account credentials

If you choose to report inappropriate, harmful, or inaccurate AI-generated content using the in-app reporting feature, we may temporarily process the reported content and selected report category to help review and improve content safety.

3. How We Use Information

User-submitted queries are processed solely for the purpose of generating AI-powered responses inspired by philosophical teachings from the Bhagavad Gita.

Queries submitted through the application may be securely transmitted to third-party AI providers in order to generate responses.

We may also process limited information related to user-submitted content reports, such as flagged responses or inappropriate output reports, to help improve application quality, safety, and moderation.

Krishna Samvaad does not use user conversations for advertising, profiling, or personalized marketing.

We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

4. AI-Generated Content

Krishna Samvaad provides AI-generated responses inspired by philosophical teachings from the Bhagavad Gita.

AI-generated responses may not always be accurate, complete, or suitable for every situation.

The application is intended for inspiration, reflection, and wellness support only and should not be considered medical, mental health, legal, financial, or professional advice.

5. Data Storage

Conversation history, saved reflections, and user interactions within Krishna Samvaad are stored locally on your device.

We do not maintain permanent cloud-based storage of personal conversations on our servers.

User queries are temporarily processed through backend infrastructure and third-party AI services solely for the purpose of generating responses.

Locally stored data may be removed at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app storage through your device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Krishna Samvaad uses trusted third-party infrastructure and AI service providers to support core application functionality.

These services may temporarily process user-submitted queries in order to generate AI-powered responses.

Third-party services used by Krishna Samvaad may include:

  • OpenAI — AI language model response generation
  • Microsoft Azure — backend hosting and infrastructure services

These providers operate under their own privacy policies and security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Krishna Samvaad is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

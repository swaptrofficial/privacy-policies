Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NEET Battle Quiz 1v1. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect information when you use our application and related services.

NEET Battle Quiz 1v1 is a real-time multiplayer quiz application designed to help students prepare for the NEET examination through competitive quiz battles, practice sessions, and performance tracking.

2. Information We Collect

NEET Battle Quiz 1v1 does not require users to create an account using an email address, phone number, or password.

To enable multiplayer gameplay, users create a simple in-app profile consisting of:

  • Display Name
  • Unique Username
  • Avatar selection (if applicable)

Your unique username is used only as an identifier for multiplayer matchmaking, live quiz battles, leaderboards, and battle history.

The application also generates an internal device identifier to maintain your local profile and support multiplayer functionality.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password or login credentials
  • Government-issued identification
  • Payment information
  • Device contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Precise device location

3. How We Use Information

The limited information collected is used solely to provide the core functionality of the application.

This includes:

  • Creating your in-app player profile
  • Identifying players during multiplayer quiz battles
  • Matchmaking with other players
  • Displaying usernames during battles and leaderboards
  • Recording battle history and performance statistics
  • Improving gameplay experience and application stability

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or personalized marketing.

We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

4. Multiplayer Gameplay

To enable real-time multiplayer battles, limited gameplay information is synchronized using Firebase Realtime Database.

This may include:

  • Username
  • Matchmaking status
  • Battle session identifier
  • Current score
  • Question progress
  • Battle results
  • Online presence during active matches

This information is used solely to facilitate multiplayer gameplay and is visible only to players participating in the same battle where necessary.

5. Data Storage

Profile information, gameplay progress, battle history, and cached quiz questions may be stored locally on your device and securely processed using cloud services required for multiplayer functionality.

Question content may also be cached locally to improve performance and reduce network usage.

You may remove locally stored data at any time by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the application.

6. Third-Party Services

NEET Battle Quiz 1v1 uses trusted third-party services to provide core application functionality.

These services may process limited information required for multiplayer gameplay, analytics, and application reliability.

Third-party services may include:

  • Firebase Realtime Database — Multiplayer synchronization and matchmaking
  • Firebase Analytics — Anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Google Play Services — Core Android platform services

These providers operate under their own privacy policies and security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

NEET Battle Quiz 1v1 is intended for students preparing for competitive examinations.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our application, legal requirements, or operational practices.

Any updates will be published within the application or on the Google Play Store listing with an updated "Last Updated" date.
